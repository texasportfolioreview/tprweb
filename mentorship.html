Mentorship

Many pathways, one goal:
a portfolio that gets you in.

Every student starts somewhere different. Whichever stage you're at, there's a track built around what you actually need next — not a generic checklist.

01

The problem

Traditional college counselors are generalists — they're rarely equipped to speak to what a strong art or architecture portfolio actually needs, because most have never built one themselves. That's a serious gap, since for these programs the portfolio isn't a supplement to the application. It is the application.

The portfolio isn't just a collection of work — it's the foundation of your narrative and a visual representation of who you are.

02

Our solution

Strong portfolios are built on real, informed guidance from people with firsthand insight into the art/architecture admissions process. Rather than recycle generic advice, our counseling stems from actual studio experience, years of research, and attendance at the nation's top universities.

Whatever your level of artistic experience, we're here to help you build, refine, and present a portfolio that's genuinely yours.

What sets it apart

Key aspects of our mentorship.

Every pathway is built around the same core principles — this is what guides every session, regardless of which one you choose.

Rooted in studio experience

Our counselors built their own art and architecture portfolios and got into the schools they were aiming for. Guidance comes from firsthand practice, not a script.

A narrative built around you

No generic templates. Every pathway is shaped around your own work, your story, and the schools you're aiming for.

Working closely with you

You work directly with the same counselor across every session — not passed between reviewers or left to a generic checklist.

Authentic, honest advice

Real critique grounded in what admissions committees actually look for — never recycled advice or feedback that's afraid to be candid.
