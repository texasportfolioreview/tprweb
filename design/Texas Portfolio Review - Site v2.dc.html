01

The problem

Traditional college counselors lack nuanced understandings of art/architecture programs, and have likely never constructed a portfolio before. That's a serious problem when it's the most important piece of your application.

The portfolio isn't just a collection of work, it's the foundation of your narrative and a visual representation of who you are.

02

Our solution

Strong portfolios are built on real, informed guidance from those with insight on the art/architecture admissions process; rather than recycle generic advice, our counseling stems from actual studio experience, years of research, and attendance at the nation's top universities.

Whatever your level of artistic experience, we are here to help you build, refine, and present your admittance-worthy portfolio.

03

Two pathways

Pathway 1
Early Start

Tailored to middle school students or high school freshmen, sophomores, and juniors looking to begin creating portfolio works.

Build

Developing a visual voice, expanding into new media, creating with real direction and cohesion.

Iterate

Honest critique of work and beginning of narrative construction.

Prepare

Outline portfolio story and place within overall application.

Pathway 2
Review & Refine

Tailored to high school seniors with a pre-existing set of work looking to compile or finalize their portfolios.

Review

Examine existing work for strengths and weaknesses, selection of works for final portfolio.

Refine

Construct portfolio narrative and revise portfolio with nuanced critiques and guidance.

Present

Finalize portfolio style, descriptions, and submission.
